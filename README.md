# Paws and Play — Doggy Daycare (iPhone Edition)

A vertical portrait iPhone game inspired by **My Mini Mart**: run a cosy doggy daycare with one thumb, stack supplies, welcome pups, unlock floor pads, and grow the place.

## iPhone IPA (`PawsAndPlay.ipa`)
- **Latest Release**: `https://github.com/AndyChenHub/doggydaycare/releases/download/latest/PawsAndPlay.ipa`
- **Local Build**: Run `python build_ipa.py` to package `PawsAndPlay.ipa`.
- **Orientation**: Portrait (`UIInterfaceOrientationPortrait`), iPhone (`UIDeviceFamily = 1`), full edge-to-edge screen with safe-area support.

## How It Plays (My Mini Mart Style)
- **One-Thumb Dynamic Virtual Joystick**: Touch and drag anywhere on the screen to steer in 360° analog direction (the joystick base follows behind your thumb for instant direction changes), or tap any spot to walk there.
- **Automatic Proximity Actions**: Every interaction triggers automatically as you walk up to it — no action buttons required.
- **Carry Stacks**: Walk onto the **Food Bin**, **Toy Box**, or **Treat Jar** pad to stack up to 3 items (or 5 with upgrades) in your hands, then jog past hungry or playful dogs to serve them in stride. Walk right over poop to scoop it into your stack and pass by the **Poop Bin** to toss it away.
- **Fast Counter Check-In & Pickup**: Step onto the **SERVE** pad behind the counter to welcome arriving owners — their dogs trot straight out to the daycare yard on their own. When an owner returns for pickup (`🏠`), walk by their dog to leash it and bring it to the **SERVE** pad for coins and tips.
- **In-World Floor Unlock Pads & Shop**: Step onto green `$Cost` floor pads in the yard to pour coins in and build new stations or hire staff on the spot, or tap **🔨 Shop** in the bottom dock to place buildings, decorations, perks, and land expansions.
